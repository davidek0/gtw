# Product

Journal and tracking system for the hospital at home ecosystem.

# Core components

At the core of the system sits the database: this keeps records of patient data.

## Data intakes

1. Wearables
1. Homepod - do a daily interview and capture trends
   (3. Doctor entry)

## Actionable insights on data

1. Quantitative algorithm: Follow deviation from norms and trends, Look at first and second derivative changes in values
1. Qualitative (AI) classifier: gaugue from non-quantitative measures how the patient is doing.

# The ultimate goals

We save on costs and eliminate unnecessary doctor visists, at the same time leveling up the way patient well-being is measured.

This enables health monitoring remotely for family, and more data-driven work for doctors.
